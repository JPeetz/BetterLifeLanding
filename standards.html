Coaching Standards & Framework

Professional Guidelines & Behavioral Science | UK English Version

BetterLife is not simply a conversational chatbot. It is a highly engineered, cognitive performance platform. Our digital architecture incorporates international coaching guidelines, cognitive-behavioral science, and habit engineering to defeat behavioral entropy and facilitate sustainable growth.

1. ICF Core Competency Alignment

BetterLife is designed to reflect the core philosophies established by the International Coaching Federation (ICF).

  • Co-Creation vs. Prescriptive Directives: In standard coaching practice, a coach acts as a sounding board rather than an autocratic advisor. BetterLife utilizes powerful, open-ended inquiry to help you articulate your own obstacles, identify cognitive assumptions, and outline personal strategies.
  • Active Active Listening & Mirroring: Aria, your AI coach, is configured to validate your emotions, summarize your complex paragraphs, and reflect key patterns back to you. This mirroring technique clarifies your thoughts and helps mitigate executive decision fatigue.
  • Maintaining Trust & Confidentiality: Trust is the foundation of growth. By storing your data strictly locally on your iPhone, BetterLife guarantees absolute physical privacy, fulfilling ICF's mandate for complete client confidentiality.

2. Cognitive Behavioral Coaching (CBC) & Procrastination Management

BetterLife utilizes proven Cognitive Behavioral Coaching (CBC) methodologies to help users restructure limiting habits:

  • Radical Task Simplification: When facing executive fatigue or ADHD symptoms, complex objectives trigger amygdala-driven threat responses, leading to chronic delay. The App instructs the AI engine to dissect large, intimidating tasks into immediate, time-bound, atomic units.
  • Obstacle Identification Matrix: For every milestone you establish, the coaching engine anticipates points of friction. We utilize structured "If X, then Y" contingency planning (e.g., *if too fatigued to paint, spend exactly four minutes organizing the tools*) to bridge the gap between intention and action.
  • Reframing Limiting Beliefs: If you struggle with setbacks, the App uses radical candor to call out cognitive patterns, gently reframing obstacles from failures into objective data metrics.

3. Habit Engineering: BJ Fogg & Atomic Habits Science

BetterLife’s goal-tracking dashboard and streak system are grounded in behavioral psychology:

We implement the **BJ Fogg Behavior Model**, which posits that behavior happens when Motivation, Ability, and a Prompt converge simultaneously ($B = MAP$):

  • Ability (Simplification): Our AI ruthlessly lowers the difficulty of execution by helping you isolate tiny, high-impact tasks.
  • Prompts (Adaptive Nudges): Precision local notifications are timed to your exact preferred timezone and circadian routines (configured during onboarding) to trigger active reflection at high-priority moments.
  • Motivation (Visually Reinforced Streaks): Daily visual streaks (represented by our custom glowing Coral flame vector icons) provide a tangible, dopaminergic reward mechanism, cementing consistent behavioral momentum.

4. Strict Boundary: Coaching vs. Clinical Therapy

In accordance with international coaching ethics, BetterLife maintains a strict and transparent operational boundary:

  • Clinical Therapy (What we do NOT do): Therapy deals with diagnosing, treating, and healing clinical psychological conditions, childhood trauma, and psychiatric illnesses. BetterLife is not a medical or therapeutic utility.
  • Behavioral Coaching (What we DO do): Coaching is future-focused, performance-driven, and active. We focus on goal clarification, habit establishment, schedule enforcement, and practical daily accountability.
