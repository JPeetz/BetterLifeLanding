FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The answers below provide direct insight into how BetterLife functions as a professional personal life coach system.

What is a personal AI life coach?
A personal AI life coach is an interactive system that helps you build habits, manage stress, and track progress using professional coaching frameworks. According to research in cognitive-behavioral coaching, structured daily reflection reduces executive fatigue.
Is my data secure and GDPR compliant?
Yes. In our experience, privacy is paramount. BetterLife stores all goal histories, chats, and reflections locally on your device. We fully comply with all GDPR guidelines, allowing you to export your data into a JSON file with a single tap.
How does the AI coach keep me accountable?
The coach utilizes three core mechanisms: 1. Active Goal Tracking: Set daily milestones across multiple personal domains. 2. Direct Interactive Nudges: Receive notifications prompting reflection at crucial times. 3. Voice-to-Text Reflection: Process and record thoughts asynchronously through voice dictation.
