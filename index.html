FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The answers below provide direct insight into how BetterLife functions as a professional personal life coach system.

What is a personal AI life coach?
A personal AI life coach is an interactive cognitive system designed to help you construct daily habits, manage stress response, and track executive progress. Grounded in cognitive-behavioral coaching principles, daily structured reflection reduces fatigue and defeats procrastination.
Is my data secure and GDPR compliant?
Absolutely. BetterLife employs a zero-tracking, local-first architecture. All chat logs, journal reflections, and metrics are saved exclusively inside the secure physical sandbox storage of your iPhone. We are fully aligned with UK GDPR, featuring built-in one-tap options to export all your records in a clean JSON format or instantly delete your profile leaving zero residue.
How does the AI coach keep me accountable?
The coach utilizes three core mechanisms: 1. Active Goal Tracking: Establish daily milestones across multiple customized focus domains. 2. Adaptive Interactive Nudges: Receive notifications prompting physical and mental check-ins timed to your timezone. 3. Voice-to-Text Reflection: Voice-type and record reflections asynchronously through our native speech dictation panel.
Why does the app require highly detailed and frequent updates?
BetterLife's self-learning cognitive loops operate under a strict input quality rule. The personalization and accuracy of your coaching feedback scale directly with the depth of the data you provide. Sharing detailed descriptions regarding your daily progress, physical recovery, mental clarity, and emotional feelings allows the AI algorithms to parse nuances, target cognitive biases, and refine accountability prompts specifically for you.
How does BetterLife differ from clinical therapy or counseling?
BetterLife is an action-oriented, future-focused coaching tool aligned with the International Coaching Federation (ICF) AI Coaching Standards—it is not a clinical medical or psychological utility. While clinical therapy diagnoses and treats mental health pathology, BetterLife is focused on cognitive performance, organizational growth, executive focus support, and habit tracking.
How does the real-time speech dictation panel work?
All speech recognition is processed locally on your physical iPhone using Apple's secure, offline-capable SFSpeechRecognizer framework. We do not stream or upload your audio files to third-party servers, guaranteeing absolute privacy for your verbal reflections.
How does the free limit work and what does premium unlock?
Your initial onboarding, goal creation, and first one (1) active coaching session are completely free of charge with no credit card required. To unlock unlimited daily sessions, advanced coaching tone adjustments, and complete metrics history, you can subscribe to our Premium monthly ($9.99) or yearly ($79.99) packages, managed securely through the Apple App Store.
