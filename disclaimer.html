Medical Disclaimer

Clinical Boundaries, AI Transparency, and Crisis Escalation Protocols

Please read this Medical Disclaimer carefully before using the BetterLife iOS application or exploring our coaching interfaces. BetterLife is built strictly as an auxiliary tool for behavioral accountability, productivity, habit optimization, and cognitive performance coaching.

🚨 If You Are in Active Crisis or Danger

BetterLife is an automated coaching tool and is strictly incapable of managing mental health emergencies, acute clinical distress, self-harm, or psychological crises. If you feel that you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please contact professional emergency responders or support helplines immediately:

United Kingdom
Call 111 / 999
Contact the Samaritans at 116 123 for free, 24/7 anonymous listening support.
United States & Canada
Call 988 / 911
The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline provides free, confidential 24/7 support.
European Union
Call 112
The universal European emergency services number, accessible free of charge.

1. Non-Medical & Non-Clinical Services Boundary

BetterLife is not a medical device, clinical service, or licensed psychiatric therapy utility.

Our automated systems and interactive interfaces do not offer medical diagnosis, medication advice, psychiatric treatment, clinical psychology consultations, or healing plans. The guidance generated by our AI modules (Aria and Aeron) represents simulated behavioral prompts designed strictly for performance coaching, cognitive restructuring, and daily productivity training.

If you are suffering from a diagnosed clinical condition—such as major clinical depression, acute general anxiety disorder, clinical ADHD, bipolar disorder, or complex PTSD—you must consult a licensed medical professional, therapist, or psychiatrist. BetterLife should not be used as a replacement for, or in contradiction to, any professional medical advice or ongoing treatment plan.

2. AI Transparency Disclosure

In strict alignment with state-level consumer protection acts and emerging AI transparency regulations, we explicitly state:

Aria and Aeron are not human beings. They are custom conversational agents powered by state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) running direct semantic inferences. While their training leverages cognitive-behavioral coaching principles, they do not possess empathy, medical intuition, or professional clinical licensing.

We do not guarantee the clinical or behavioral outcome of any goal, routine, or cognitive reframing exercise. The suggestions are executed entirely at the user's personal discretion.
