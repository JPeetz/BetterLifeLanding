Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I cancel or manage my premium subscription?
Since all payments are processed securely through Apple, you can manage or cancel your BetterLife subscription directly in your iPhone settings. Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > BetterLife, and select "Cancel Subscription".
How do I permanently delete my account and data?
You can easily delete your account from within the app. Open BetterLife, go to your Profile Settings, and scroll down to the red "Delete Account" button. Please note that this action is irreversible and all your journal entries and AI coaching history will be permanently erased from our servers in compliance with GDPR.
Is my journal data read by human employees?
Absolutely not. Your privacy is our top priority. Your conversations with the BetterLife AI Coach are securely encrypted and processed by our secure API. We do not use your personal entries to train public AI models, and our employees do not have access to read your journals.
I found a bug or technical issue. How do I report it?
We appreciate your help in making BetterLife better! Please email us at support@betterlife-technologies.co.uk with a brief description of the issue and your device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro).
